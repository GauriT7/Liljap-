Getting around

The transport rules that save stress.

IC card

Use Suica / PASMO / ICOCA for most local trains, metros and convenience-store taps. Keep intercity reservations separate.

Fuji transfer

9 Dec: Shinjuku → Kawaguchiko on the reserved FUJI EXCURSION. 10 Dec: bus to Mishima, then Shinkansen to Kyoto. Leave a generous connection buffer.

Big luggage

Consider forwarding your large suitcase Tokyo → Kyoto and carry a small overnight bag to Fuji. Confirm the hotel accepts deliveries.

Airport morning

14 Dec: early Namba → KIX service is the target. Recheck the live Nankai timetable and have a taxi fallback.
